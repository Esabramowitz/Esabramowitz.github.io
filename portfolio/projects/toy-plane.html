Toy Plane Reverse Engineering

Back to projects

This CAD modeling project reverse-engineers a toy propeller airplane in SolidWorks by recreating its geometry as a fully assembled digital model while preserving the original toy’s appearance. The project decomposes the aircraft into seven unique components—including the fuselage, wings, engine, propeller, landing gear, wheels, and cockpit—and uses a combination of revolves, lofts, boundary surfaces, patterns, and assembly techniques to reconstruct the design while applying practical geometric simplifications. The final model successfully captures the toy’s overall form and functionality, with the project highlighting the challenges of complex surface modeling and assembly sequencing, particularly in the cockpit geometry and lofted aerodynamic features.
